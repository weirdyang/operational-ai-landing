# Operational AI Modernization Landing Page

Single-page landing page to validate the idea of AI modernization of operational portals for Singapore mid-market SMEs (precision engineering, logistics, B2B professional services).

## Usage
- Open `index.html` locally or host it.
- Quick free hosting: [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop), Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, or GitHub Pages.
- Replace the form `action` in `index.html` with a real endpoint (Formspree, Netlify Forms, etc.) to capture leads.

## Validation tips
- Drive traffic via LinkedIn outreach to ICP contacts, SPETA/SLA networks, or targeted ads.
- Track form submissions and follow up with free assessments.
- Focus conversations on operational pain, current tools, and willingness to pay / grant interest.

Created for idea testing. Outcome-focused messaging (not stack-specific).
